About Motify

Web-native motion graphics, kept editable.

Motify is a code-first motion graphics editor for creating customizable animation projects with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and GSAP. Author the source, refine it on a canvas, adjust the timeline, and render the result locally.

The problem

Teams often need short product videos, feature launch animations, social clips, and SVG motion graphics that feel specific to their product. Motify keeps those compositions editable instead of final and frozen.

The workflow

Start with an HTML composition, inspect the result visually, move elements on the canvas, tune timing on the GSAP timeline, then keep iterating in source. The project stays local and version-friendly.
