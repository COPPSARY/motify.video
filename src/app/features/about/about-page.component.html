Motify turns what you ship into content that ships

Motify is the product marketing content workflow for SaaS teams. We turn product updates and marketing goals into accurate, on-brand, production-ready motion graphics and launch assets.

“Teams should be able to focus on, and improve their products, Motify will handle announcing their great products”

— Reaksa, Founder

Product teams should not need a production department for every story.

SaaS teams ship continuously, but the content around each launch rarely moves at the same speed. A feature may be ready while its explainer, announcement, demo, and campaign variations are still waiting on a long creative handoff. Small teams feel that gap most: the people who understand the product are also the people writing the brief, collecting screenshots, reviewing edits, and preparing launch day.

Motify is being built to close that gap. It gives founders, marketers, and product teams a direct path from product context to an editable video. The aim is not to remove creative judgment. It is to let the people closest to the product use that judgment on the audience, message, proof, and final direction instead of spending it on repetitive production mechanics.

Ground every first draft in something real.

  1. 01

    Bring the product context

    Start with the goal, audience, product link, screenshots, proof points, and the action the viewer should take.

  2. 02

    Set the brand direction

    Use Brand DNA to carry forward the logo, colors, typography, voice, visual style, and audience your team already knows.

  3. 03

    Plan and generate the story

    Approve the visual flow with a storyboard when needed, then generate an editable sequence built around the brief.

  4. 04

    Refine what matters

    Adjust scenes, copy, timing, pacing, and formats so the final video remains accurate as the product and campaign evolve.
