Questions before you start?

How many credits does a video use?

A typical generation uses about 10 credits, and a complete video commonly takes around three generations. Length, complexity, revisions, and model usage affect the final cost.

Do credits expire?

Plan credits expire on the date attached to each plan grant. Welcome credits and separately purchased credit packs do not expire. Motify spends credits with the nearest expiry first.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Renewing the same active plan extends it. Switching to a different plan starts the new plan immediately, so remaining access time on the previous plan is not carried over.

Does my plan renew automatically?

No. Bakong KHQR purchases are one-time payments. Renew by creating a new checkout when you want another plan period.

Can I use the videos commercially?

Yes. Your finished Motify videos can be used for product launches, social content, campaigns, and other commercial work.
